Industry & Projects

Applied work

Twenty years of building and leading analytics inside global banks, consulting firms and technology companies, and — since 2024 — applied projects led from BIM for industry partners.

Applied projects led from BIM (2024 – present)

  • Lookalike-audience AI framework to optimise target audience — Accusaga Inc. and Health Union, US (2024). Audience modelling under explicit responsible-AI constraints on which attributes may be used.
  • AI use cases in banking and financial services — Aspire Systems, Singapore and India (around two years, 2024–2026). Framing and building use cases across eight financial institutions in Australia, the US, the UK and India, with model-risk and regulatory constraints treated as part of the design.
  • Net impact of e-commerce on employment and consumer welfare in India — Pahle India Foundation and Flipkart (2024).
  • Shop-floor quality and reliability prediction — PQSI Digital, for cement, steel and auto-component plants. Includes the sensor-free predictive-maintenance study at an integrated special-steel plant.
  • WeldVision AI — computer-vision process monitoring for arc welding on a plant's existing cameras, with one of India's pioneering and oldest welding research organisations (in progress).
  • Sūtradhāra — BIM's governed agentic-AI framework for the student lifecycle (implementation from 2026–27).

About

Sridevi Tandley

Assistant Professor, Analytics & Economics · Chair, Centre for Industrial Consultancy · Chair, Centre for Artificial Intelligence & Decision Science (CAIDS) — Bharathidasan Institute of Management, Tiruchirappalli · Founder, SIJI Consultancy LLP

I am an economist who spent two decades building AI and advanced-analytics capability inside global banks, consulting firms and international research organisations, and now teach and lead applied research and industry work at BIM Tiruchirappalli.

My research sits where economics meets machine learning: how many indicators a GDP nowcast should use and why the answer depends on a country's structure; what India's Phillips curve looks like once demand and supply shocks are separated, read through a large language model; and how agentic AI should be governed when it moves from predicting to acting — in loan origination, on steel-plant maintenance floors, and inside a business school's own student lifecycle.

Before academia I led data-science teams at Mashreq Bank, Citibank, WNS and Tata Consultancy Services, and delivered analytics for clients across banking, insurance, retail, technology, pharma and healthcare in eight countries. I hold a Ph.D. in Economics from the Centre for Economic Studies & Planning, Jawaharlal Nehru University, and received the Data & AI ICON 2022 award (Future.AI, BFSI category). Since February 2024 I have also been the Founder of SIJI Consultancy LLP, an AI and analytics firm that bridges the gap between theoretical research and real-world application.

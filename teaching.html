Courses Designed & Offered

Every course is built as hands-on, project-based learning — case exercises, graded assignments and rubric-based evaluation — with governance taught inside the technical material rather than as a separate ethics module.

Advanced Predictive AnalyticsForecasting, classification and validation discipline — out-of-time testing, calibration, fairness audit — on enterprise-grade data.
Artificial Intelligence & Machine LearningSupervised and unsupervised learning, model interpretation and model-risk reasoning for managers.
Generative AI for BusinessLarge language and multimodal models as managerial instruments: prompt design, retrieval grounding, output validation, disclosure.
Data Science ToolsThe Python data-science stack from raw data to a defensible finding.
Business AnalyticsFraming business questions as analytical problems and interpreting the answers in context.
Applied Marketing AnalyticsSegmentation, marketing-mix and ROI modelling, customer lifetime value and recommender logic.
Business EconomicsEconomic reasoning for managers — markets, macro conditions and policy as they bear on firm decisions.
Digital Product & Service ManagementDesigning and managing digital products and services, from discovery to metrics and iteration.

Emerging-topic modules

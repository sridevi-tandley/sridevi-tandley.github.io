Research

Papers and projects

Three threads run through the work: how much information an economic forecast really needs, what India's inflation dynamics look like once supply shocks are separated from demand, and how AI systems that act — not just predict — should be governed.

Working papers

  • 2026Digital Public Infrastructure and Bayesian Nowcasting of India's GDP: Why a Few Timely Indicators Suffice Tandley Omprakash Sridevi and Karan Singh Bagavathinathan · SSRN working paper, this version August 2026 Tests whether adding indicators can worsen a nowcast when the reliable sample is short. In a Bayesian state-space ensemble on Indian quarterly data, a compact set is optimal; a five-indicator workhorse (RMSE 0.74) and the preferred specification (0.72) both beat the RBI Survey of Professional Forecasters (1.03). The incremental gain from the UPI/GST digital layer is positive but imprecise, with a pre-registered re-test in 2028 and 2032. SSRN 6897978
  • 2026Know the Economy, Not the Data Lake: Country Structure and the Right Number of Indicators in a GDP Nowcast Karan Singh Bagavathinathan and Tandley Omprakash Sridevi · SSRN working paper, this version July 2026 Writes the indicator-count choice as a bias–variance trade-off and tests it on twelve economies over a common 2000–2026 out-of-sample window. The accuracy-maximising count is smaller in emerging economies (mean 3.2 against 6.4) and is explained by economic complexity rather than income — with Norway as the identifying case. SSRN 7127798
  • 2026Inflation Targeting in the Time of Supply Shocks: A Large-Language-Model Reading of India's Reserve Bank, 1997–2025 Karan Singh Bagavathinathan and Tandley Omprakash Sridevi · SSRN working paper, this draft July 2026 India's Phillips curve looks flat across 1997–2025. Conditioning on the shock, the demand-pull slope is +2.2 before inflation targeting and zero after it, while the supply slope stays negative — the signature of a central bank offsetting demand before it reaches prices. A large language model reading the RBI's own assessments, blind to the output gap, recovers the same regime contrast across three model families. SSRN 6970159
